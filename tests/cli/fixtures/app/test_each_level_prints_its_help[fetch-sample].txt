Usage: thermur fetch sample

Fetches the WRF-SFIRE sample into the clone's data/ folder.                     

Downloads the archive at the pinned revision of the Hugging Face dataset, checks
it against the pinned SHA-256 digest, and extracts it, then prints the path of  
the output the graph reads, relative to the clone, which the simulation setting 
takes. Exits nonzero, extracting nothing, where the download fails or the       
archive's digest differs from the pinned one.
