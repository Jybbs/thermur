# The Lookout

The Lookout plays a flight of *Thermur*'s composite, named for the tower a fire crew watches the sky from. A murmuration runs to thousands of birds, a mean of 30,082 across more than 3,000 counted flocks[^goodenough2017], so the slider sets the flock from 20 drones to 3,000, its default of 1,000 marked on the track, and a size let go flies the flight again from the same seed. Beside the flight sit the drone count, the time the flight has flown, the flock's polarization, from zero for headings that cancel to one for a flock flying as one, and the number of groups it splits into, two drones sharing a group where a chain of hops no longer than three of the launch spacings joins them.

The drawing below flies the flock in the page under the TypeScript copy of the default composite the rules page's drawing flies, seen from the side with east across and height up. `thermur watch` opens the same drawing in a browser on a local address, playing instead the frames the package flies through its graph at the size the slider sets.
