# Results

The pages in this section set what the flock does when it flies beside what was measured on starling flocks and on outdoor drone flocks, so a claim about the flock rests on a statistic *Thermur* computes across flock sizes and seeds. Each measurement a page cites is declared once as a norm in `thermur.metrics`, and each figure a page states comes from a run of the pipeline, named with its sizes, its seeds, and its length.

- [The Flock in Still Air](still-air.md) flies the flock at six sizes from 10 to 3,000 drones through air with neither fire nor wind, setting each statistic beside its norm and each scaling against the flock's size

`thermur fly sweep` flies the sweep and records it under `data/runs/`, and `mise measure` writes the means the figures draw out of that run.
