# Thermur

*A starling murmuration that breaks around a wildfire the way it breaks around a falcon.*

*Thermur*, a portmanteau of thermal and murmuration, is research code for a flock of drones that flies as a starling murmuration and treats a wildfire as its predator. The air the flock flies through comes from the [plume simulations](https://doi.org/10.20383/102.0314) Moisseeva and Stull ran in WRF-SFIRE, which couples a wildfire to the Weather Research and Forecasting model (WRF). The code is on [GitHub](https://github.com/Jybbs/thermur).
